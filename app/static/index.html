What each state means

  • Full — Looks like the full shelf. No action.
  • Low — Some items are missing. Check soon.
  • Gap — A clear empty area. Restock now.
  • Check shelf — Lighting or camera moved. Do not trust this reading; fix the camera or lighting, then re-capture if the shelf is full.

Alerts appear here only — no messages are sent anywhere else. A gap or low state held for a few frames in a row raises one alert banner.

ShelfScan is for one fixed camera on one shelf. Frames are processed on the server and are not stored, unless you save a snapshot. Counts are estimates, not stock counts.
