ShelfScan

One shelf, one camera, one area. Baseline a full shelf, then watch Full / Low / Gap / Check shelf. Demo mode needs no webcam.

Model: yolo26n.onnx · Inference runs on the server, not in your browser. (OpenCV version shown by /health.)

Shelf states (from baseline drift, not from the item count): Full — changed under 10%, no action. Low — changed 10–30%, check soon. Gap — changed 30% or more, restock now. Check shelf — large brightness/camera shift, do not trust the state; re-check lighting and camera, then re-capture the baseline if the shelf is full.

Restock flow: 1) Restock the shelf fully. 2) Click “Capture baseline (full shelf) — use after restocking” with the webcam running. 3) The state should return to Full. There is no separate restock button — re-baselining is the restock confirmation, and it also clears any active alert. Alerts are in-dashboard only: a Gap or Low state sustained for 5 consecutive frames shows a banner here; no SMS, email, push, or sound notification is sent.
