What each state means

✅

Full

The shelf looks like its full baseline. No action needed.

⚠️

Low

Some items are missing. Check soon.

🚨

Gap

A clear empty area. Restock now.

🔍

Check shelf

Lighting changed or the camera moved. Do not trust the state — fix the camera or lighting, then re-capture the baseline.

One journey, start to finish

  1. Point a fixed camera at one shelf and capture a “full” baseline.
  2. ShelfScan compares each new frame with that baseline.
  3. A Gap or Low held for 5 frames raises an in-dashboard alert. No SMS, email or push alert in this version.
  4. Restock fully, capture a new baseline, and the state returns to Full. That re-baseline is the restock confirmation.

Counting, honestly

ShelfScan also gives a rough product count estimate — one class, “product”, no brand or barcode. On 5 held-out real shelf photos it scored MAE 7.0 (predicted 146 vs hand-counted 175): cookie tins 19/20, lemonade 91/88, wooden bottles 14/16, soda cans 17/27, bleach 5/24 (white-on-white, worst case).

Estimate, not a stock count. Do not use it for stock or ordering. Counting does not run on this demo-only page.

What this page cannot do

  • No webcam, no photo upload, no baseline capture, no restock action here.
  • No OpenCV, no YOLO inference, no annotated image here.
  • Fixed camera, one shelf, one area only. No multi-shelf, SKU, barcode or shop till link.
  • No shop pilot yet, and no real physical before/after removal pair yet. Gap proof is a simulated whole-row removal on a real photo, plus a constructed stills video.
